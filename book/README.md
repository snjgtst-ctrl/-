# Yokai Night Watch — series index

Fixed-layout Kindle picture books for children ages 2-5, sold on international Amazon.
A bedtime first-aid kit, not a classic: the buyer is a parent who is out of road at 9pm.

**The spine of the series:** every night, a different yokai takes the night watch — so the
child sleeps, and the exhausted grown-up is allowed to sleep first.

## Format rules every volume holds

1. The child never leaves the futon.
2. The yokai takes over the watch and always says it will guard **the big warm one**
   beside the child too.
3. Word count falls page by page; the last page lands on a three-word "…Goodnight." form.
4. The STYLE and FORMAT prompt blocks are byte-identical across volumes.
5. No moralizing. No "be good", no "go to sleep now". No gore, no genuinely scary faces.

## Recurring cast

**Momo**, three years old, is the same child in every volume. Reusing her halves the art
cost per book, gives returning readers something to recognize, and makes the promise to
the parent into a brand.

## Open items

- Book 1 is 14 pages, Book 2 is 12. Trimming Book 1 to 12 would align production, pricing
  and the series listing.
- Covers are not designed. Covers must **not** reuse the interior's dark, low-contrast
  look — an Amazon thumbnail is about a centimeter square and the interior palette
  disappears at that size. Covers need their own brighter, higher-contrast treatment.
